# NFL player availability foundation and Phase 2B yardage slice

The resolver and original Week 7 preview established the offline foundation. Phase 2B adds a manually verified official capture and makes the current-week yardage generator consume it for candidate eligibility and QB starter choice. It changes no model formula or archive contract. The Week 7 preview has been regenerated from the checked capture ledger, removing its earlier inferred Bears UTC publication time.

Before Phase 2B, sources did not form a shared current-week availability authority. `src/lib/fantasy/weekly/availability.ts` and DFS role context are partial reusable helpers, while yardage and TD made separate choices. The 2026 nflverse injury cache is absent (`data/nfl/nflverse/injuries/manifest.json` ends in 2025). The 2026 weekly roster was retrieved 2026-10-22, but its manifest records only the date, not a publication instant. The depth-chart cache was retrieved 2026-10-22 and records source snapshot `2026-10-22T12:59:29Z`; it originates with ESPN. The cached source projects only the latest daily depth snapshot, so it cannot reconstruct prior daily role states. Historical player usage is strictly prior-game evidence for projection features, not starter confirmation. The official team pages display publication times without an explicit timezone, so the capture records null UTC publication times.

The reported **McCarthy versus Winston** case is a cross-product inconsistency, not a current yardage misassignment: the Week 7 yardage artifact generated 2026-10-22 18:06Z projects McCarthy at NYG QB depth rank 1, while fantasy's 2026-10-20 13:23Z artifact contains both quarterbacks with no starter label. The [Giants' 2026-10-22 article](https://www.giants.com/news/j-j-mccarthy-to-start-quarterback-new-york-giants-week-7-houston-texans-john-harbaugh-jameis-winston) confirms McCarthy starts and Winston backs up. The page displays Oct 22 at 12:43 PM without a verifiable timezone; its exact publication instant cannot be compared to the fantasy cutoff. That artifact must not be retroactively called wrong at its cutoff.

The **Monangai OUT** case is a confirmed live defect: the [Bears' 2026-10-21 injury update](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/injury-update-bears-rg-jonah-jackson-set-to-play-vs-patriots) rules him out for Week 7, while the 2026-10-22 18:06Z yardage artifact projects rushing and receiving yards and the 18:45Z TD artifact includes him. The Week 7 weekly-roster row says ACT despite Week 5/6 INA rows, showing ACT is not a current health determination. Fantasy's earlier Tuesday artifact also contains Monangai, but predates the final OUT announcement. The stale injury artifact cannot correct either product.

## Resolution contract

Records are keyed by season, week, canonical game ID and `gsis:` player ID, with team checked on every evidence item and opponent derived from the canonical game. Missing/ambiguous identity produces unknown. An accepted fact must have a valid retrieval timestamp at or before the prediction cutoff. Publication time is UTC when independently verifiable and otherwise null; when present it cannot exceed retrieval. The cutoff must precede kickoff. Future, malformed, cross-team and cross-game facts are rejected with reasons. ESPN depth evidence older than 48 hours is stale and rejected. Rejected facts never silently influence a result.

Priority within each fact kind is official team/NFL statement, nflverse injury report, weekly roster, ESPN depth chart, then historical usage. More recent publication wins within a priority. Role and health are independent. An explicit team starter/backup announcement is stronger than depth rank even if ESPN refreshes later. Depth rank 1 alone means **projected**, never confirmed, QB starter. An OUT designation or inactive list excludes lineup eligibility. Questionable/doubtful is conditional. Missing game status, and roster ACT, remain unknown. Backup/rotation does not imply an exact share or automatic promotion. Multiple RB/WR/TE players can share a role or start in different personnel groups; the schema has no forced RB1/WR1/WR2 field. A conflict flag and evidence IDs explain disagreements. Availability confidence is distinct from any snap, usage or fantasy estimate.

The offline preview is `docs/research/nfl-availability-week7-preview.json`, generated by `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/preview-nfl-availability-week7.ts --output=docs/research/nfl-availability-week7-preview.json`. It compares three canonical identities against existing yardage and fantasy artifacts, preserves input hashes and official evidence URLs, and has a deterministic snapshot ID. Its cutoff is 2026-10-22 21:11Z, after the checked retrieval at 21:10:43Z. Publication UTC timestamps are null because the official pages display times without a verifiable timezone. There is no official game-day inactive list yet. The resolver does not infer which CHI backup receives Monangai's work.

## Phase 2B current-week yardage integration

`data/nfl/availability/2026/week-07.json` is a versioned factual ledger checked against public Bears and Giants pages on 2026-10-22 at 21:10:43Z. It records canonical game/team/GSIS identity, URL, attribution, retrieved time, null UTC publication time, observed designation or role, and explicit partial coverage. It contains factual classifications rather than article text. `capture.ts` rejects malformed, conflicting, mismatched, and post-cutoff captures before generator publication. A capture older than 72 hours is marked stale. If it contains an official OUT or inactive fact, the generator fails before publication so the previously excluded player cannot silently reenter active rows. A stale capture without an exclusion retains legacy candidates, but every row is marked availability unknown. A missing ledger also retains legacy candidates with unknown availability and explicit missing coverage. A partial ledger never means unlisted players are healthy. There is no automated official-page fetch in this slice. An editor must recheck and update the ledger, including its actual retrieval time, before the 72-hour limit. Game-day inactives use a separate `inactive` fact when verified; none were available at this capture.

The yardage generator resolves current roster candidates against that ledger. OUT/inactive candidates are removed before QB choice and from published rows; the receiving allocator retains their prior opportunity share as unallocated residual, so other players do not inherit speculative targets. The existing scarcity rule can consider other ACT candidates without adding a usage estimate. A confirmed official QB starter wins over ESPN depth rank. An announced backup cannot win the QB heuristic. Emitted rows carry an `availability` block, including `unknown` when no relevant status exists. The existing Week 7 yardage artifact has 403 rows; an offline dry run at 2026-10-22T21:11:00Z has 401. The only removals are Kyle Monangai rushing and receiving; the only role-source change is J.J. McCarthy passing from ESPN depth to the official Giants announcement. Jameis Winston remains absent as a passing candidate. D'Andre Swift was already projected, so no replacement row or invented carry allocation was added. The eight other Bears receiving rows retain exactly their previous target and yard projections. All 401 emitted rows retain unknown game availability because no other final report was captured; McCarthy's starter role is independently confirmed.

TD Scorer's current producer takes only projected QB passing, RB rushing, and WR/TE receiving rows from the published yardage artifact, then deduplicates by player ID. It has no separate availability check. A future TD run reading a published Week 7 v2 yardage artifact would therefore omit Monangai from its candidate population; its score normalization could change for other players. This is an existing input dependency, not a TD model edit. The already published TD artifact is untouched in this PR, and the Week 7 dry-run regression verifies that the proposed yardage input has no Monangai rushing or receiving row. TD production should be reviewed before any v2 yardage artifact is published.

The current-week row schema advances to `nfl-current-week-yardage-projection-v2` for the availability and source-coverage fields; the validator still accepts historical v1 artifacts. With a validated official capture, the new eligibility population uses passing/rushing model suffix `v2` and receiving-share `v3.0.0` per the Model Versioning Guide. These names describe changed candidate population and role selection, while fitted coefficients and yardage equations stay fixed. A missing capture retains previous model versions and candidate selection, with v2 rows marked unknown. Historical replay retains the v1 row contract.

Preview command: `npx tsx scripts/generate-nfl-current-week-yardage-projections.ts --season=2026 --week=7 --generated-at=2026-10-22T21:11:00Z --dry-run --output=<temporary path>`. Dry run writes only the explicit preview file, with no prediction archive or public artifact write. A normal run consumes the same ledger before publication; this implementation has not run that normal path.

## Remaining integration sequence

1. Expand weekly official fact coverage and maintain the source ledger before each production run. Capture official game-day inactives as they become available. A future automated fetch may record response hashes and verifiable publication metadata; the current manual ledger does not claim either when unavailable. Do not rely on the mutable latest depth cache for historical replay.
2. Review the Phase 2B yardage preview and source coverage before enabling a production run. Preserve numerical projection formulas and historical outputs.
3. Revisit fantasy's documented injury exclusion with an explicit model/policy version and approval. Apply availability to eligibility/display without changing frozen coefficients or reinterpreting older ranks.
4. Map resolver OUT/inactive/conditional/unknown and role evidence into DFS optimizer policy; retain DK upload status as a separate contest signal and preserve existing projection/usage thresholds.
5. Filter TD candidates through the same game-week snapshot after yardage, with an explicit missing-snapshot behavior. Recompute the score population only in a new version.

No paid API is required. Source coverage remains incomplete: official team reports need a maintained capture process; nflverse's 2026 injury release is missing here; current injury display is stale; roster release timestamps have day precision; ESPN depth data is mutable and secondary; DraftKings status has no observation timestamp; and licensing/redistribution terms for future official-site capture should be reviewed before publishing raw content. Store links and factual classifications rather than copying article text. A future automated fetch can add source-payload hashes where permitted.
